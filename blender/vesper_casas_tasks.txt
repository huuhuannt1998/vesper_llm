# VESPER-CASAS Task Alignment and Dataset Generation Guide

## CASAS Dataset Structure (from Zenodo)

### 📋 **5 Core ADL Tasks**:
1. **t1: Make a phone call** 
   - Move to phone in dining room
   - Look up number in phone book
   - Dial and listen to cooking directions
   - Summarize on notepad

2. **t2: Wash hands**
   - Move to kitchen sink
   - Wash hands with soap
   - Dry with paper towel

3. **t3: Cook** 
   - Cook oatmeal per phone directions
   - Measure water, boil in pot
   - Add oats, serve with raisins and brown sugar

4. **t4: Eat**
   - Take oatmeal and medicine to dining room
   - Eat the food

5. **t5: Clean**
   - Take dishes to sink
   - Clean with water and dish soap

### 🏠 **CASAS Sensor Types**:
- **M01-M026**: PIR motion detectors (ON/OFF)
- **I01-I08**: Item sensors (PRESENT/ABSENT)
  - I01: oatmeal, I02: raisins, I03: brown sugar, I04: bowl
  - I05: measuring spoon, I06: medicine, I07: pot, I08: phone book
- **D01**: Kitchen cabinet door (OPEN/CLOSE)
- **AD1-A/B**: Water sensors (level values)
- **AD1-C**: Burner sensor (level values)
- **asterisk**: Phone use sensor

### 📊 **Dataset Structure**:
- **120 files**: adl_noerror (20 participants × 5 tasks + 20 re-dos)
- **100 files**: adl_error (20 participants × 5 tasks, with scripted errors)
- **Format**: `p{participant}.t{task}.csv` (e.g., p01.t1.csv)

## Current VESPER vs CASAS Gap

### 🎯 **Current VESPER Tasks** (vesper_tasks.txt):
```
Go to bedroom|Cook in kitchen|Rest in bedroom
```

### ❌ **Alignment Issues**:
1. **Task Mismatch**: VESPER has generic room navigation, CASAS has specific ADL sequences
2. **Device Mismatch**: VESPER has no specific item interactions, CASAS tracks detailed object use
3. **Sequence Mismatch**: VESPER tasks are room-based, CASAS tasks are activity-based
4. **Sensor Mismatch**: Need to map Blender rooms to CASAS sensor locations
