Ultra-compact (1 paragraph):
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
We implemented a CASAS-compatible interaction system with 19 binary
item sensors and 11 virtual IoT devices that activate based on 
proximity (1.0-1.5m) and task semantics. A temporal acceleration
mechanism compresses virtual time while maintaining accurate 
timestamps, enabling 8 hours of sleep to execute in 5 seconds (5760×)
and 10 minutes of cooking in 3 seconds (200×). The system exports
CASAS-format logs with millisecond precision.
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Inline statistics:
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
The system implements 19 CASAS-compatible item sensors distributed 
across five rooms (Kitchen: 7, Dining: 2, Bathroom: 4, Bedroom: 3,
Living: 3) and 11 virtual IoT devices with task-based automation.
Time acceleration ranges from 30× (hand washing) to 5760× (8-hour
sleep), enabling 41 minutes of virtual activity to execute in 13
seconds while maintaining millisecond-precision timestamps.
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
